Our Story

About CosVanta

Become the Character.

CosVanta is a student-created dummy brand developed as part of an academic digital marketing project. The concept focuses on cosplay, fandom culture and character-inspired fashion.

Our mission is to create an online brand concept where fans can discover costume ideas and accessories while expressing their own creativity.

What We Stand For

  • Original character-inspired concepts
  • Strong visual storytelling
  • Complete cosplay looks
  • Community interaction through digital content
  • Clear distinction between concepts and licensed products
